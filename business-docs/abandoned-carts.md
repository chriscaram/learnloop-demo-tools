# LearnLoop: Q4 2026 Business Review

**Author:** Head of Product
**Date:** 15 November 2026
**Audience:** Leadership team and external advisors

## 1. Where we are

LearnLoop is an online course platform that people pay for monthly. One plan, 19 USD per month, gives access to the whole catalog: 12 self-paced courses in four areas (Data, Cloud, Leadership, Design), each made of six to ten short video lessons with quizzes.

Learners pick their next course in the store: they put it in their shopping cart and complete the checkout, which enrols them (the course is included in their plan). The cart is saved to their account, so it follows them from one device to another.

We started September with about **420 paying learners**, about 7,980 USD in monthly recurring revenue. We did not run any acquisition activity this autumn, so the business today depends on the learners we already have, and on them moving on from one course to the next.

The team that runs the service day to day (learner support, account questions, course operations) is **three people**. Product and engineering are a separate, small group.

Comparing the two most recent 30-day periods (17 Sep to 16 Oct, then 17 Oct to 15 Nov):

- **Learners who completed a checkout:** 320 → 172 (down about 46%). Until early October almost everyone who put a course in the cart completed the checkout the same day. Within about ten days in mid-October that changed, and it has not recovered.
- **Learners who put a course in their cart:** 327 → 312. Interest has not fallen: about as many people choose a next course as before.
- **Courses waiting in carts:** on 15 November, **147 learners** have courses in their cart that they never checked out, 640 courses in total. Some of them have been waiting for more than a month.
- **Learners who opened a lesson:** about 330 every week, unchanged. But **lessons completed** went from about 880 a week to about 550 a week (down about a third): learners keep coming back, yet fewer of them have a new course to work on.
- **Cancellations** stayed at about 4 a week. Active subscriptions on 15 November: 386.

Price, plan, catalog and the checkout page stayed the same during this period. Service health was stable: we had no outages, error rates stayed very low and page speed did not change. Our regular releases went out every two weeks or so, as usual.

## 3. What worries us

1. **Learners are not completing what they start in the store.** Nearly half of the learners who choose their next course now leave it in the cart.
2. **Progress is slowing down.** A learner who does not enrol in the next course has nothing new to learn. Lessons completed are down a third, and we expect cancellations to follow if this continues.
3. **We do not yet understand why.** Nothing we changed on purpose explains it.
4. **Our capacity is limited.** Three people already handle support and operations. We cannot follow up with 147 learners one by one.

## 4. Priorities for Q4 2026

In order of importance:

1. **Complete the purchases learners start.** At least 300 learners completing a checkout every 30 days by the end of the quarter, which is where we were in September (it is 172 now), and no course left waiting in a cart for more than 7 days.
2. **Keep our subscribers.** Keep weekly cancellations at about 4 a week or lower.
3. **More learners actively progressing every week.** Get lessons completed back above 850 a week.

**Guardrail:** support workload must not grow. The three-person team should not handle more tickets, messages or manual tasks per week than it does today. We will judge any change against this as well as against the three goals above.

## 5. What we are asking for

We want an independent view of what is driving these numbers and which outcomes are realistic for this quarter. We are not committing to any particular approach yet. Please look at the evidence first.
