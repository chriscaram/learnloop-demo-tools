# LearnLoop: Q4 2026 Business Review

**Author:** Head of Product
**Date:** 15 November 2026
**Audience:** Leadership team and external advisors

## 1. Where we are

LearnLoop is an online course platform that people pay for monthly. One plan, 19 USD per month, gives access to the whole catalog: 12 self-paced courses in four areas (Data, Cloud, Leadership, Design), each made of six to ten short video lessons with quizzes. Learners pick their next course in the store (cart and checkout, included in their plan) and can ask the team questions from the Help page.

We started September with about **420 paying learners**, about 7,980 USD in monthly recurring revenue. We did not run any acquisition activity this autumn, so the business today depends on the learners we already have.

The team that runs the service day to day (learner support, account questions, course operations) is **three people**. Product and engineering are a separate, small group.

## 3. What we watch

1. **Retention.** With no new learners coming in, every cancellation is lost revenue we do not replace.
2. **Progress.** Learners who keep finishing lessons and courses stay with us.
3. **Our capacity.** Three people handle support and operations. Anything that adds manual work for them will not be sustainable.

## 4. Priorities for Q4 2026

In order of importance:

1. **Keep our subscribers.** Weekly cancellations at about 4 a week or lower by the end of the quarter.
2. **More learners actively progressing every week.** At least 300 learners completing or starting lessons each week, and courses finished per month above 450.
3. **Complete the purchases learners start.** At least 300 learners completing a checkout every 30 days, and no course left waiting in a cart for more than 7 days.
4. **Answer every learner question within one business day.** Measured by our morning check, which flags each question still waiting after one business day: target 0 flagged a week.

**Guardrail:** support workload must not grow. The three-person team should not handle more tickets, messages or manual tasks per week than it does today. We will judge any change against this as well as against the goals above.

## 5. What we are asking for

We want an independent view of these numbers and of which outcomes are realistic for this quarter. We are not committing to any particular approach yet. Please look at the evidence first.
