# LearnLoop: Q4 2026 Business Review

**Author:** Head of Product
**Date:** 15 November 2026
**Audience:** Leadership team and external advisors

## 1. Where we are

LearnLoop is an online course platform that people pay for monthly. One plan, 19 USD per month, gives access to the whole catalog: 12 self-paced courses in four areas (Data, Cloud, Leadership, Design), each made of six to ten short video lessons with quizzes.

Learners pick their next course in the course catalog: they put it in their shopping cart and complete the checkout, which enrols them in the course (the course is included in their plan). The cart is saved to their account, so it follows them from one device to another.

Learners who need help use the Help page: they ask the team a question there and come back to it to read the answer.

We started September with about **420 paying learners**, about 7,980 USD in monthly recurring revenue. We did not run any acquisition activity this autumn, so the business today depends on the learners we already have.

**Enterprise customers.** 235 of our learners are employees of three enterprise customers: Northwind Logistics (95 employees), Helix Health Partners (80) and Corvel Bank (60). Each of them requires its employees to complete certain courses by a deadline, and expects us to report who has and who has not. These accounts matter beyond their size: they are how we expect to grow next year.

The team that runs the service day to day (learner support, account questions, course operations, and following up on required training) is **three people**. They work from the Admin area. Product and engineering are a separate, small group.

Comparing the two most recent 30-day periods (17 Sep to 16 Oct, then 17 Oct to 15 Nov):

**Learning**

- **Learners actively taking lessons:** 340 → 195 (down about 43%). The fall happened over roughly two weeks from early October, then levelled off; it has not recovered. Most of the learners who stopped still sign in every few days and look at their dashboard and the catalog.
- **Lessons completed:** 3,483 → 1,864 (down 46%).

**The store**

- **Learners who completed a checkout:** 316 → 158 (down 50%).
- **Learners who put a course in their cart:** 332 → 290. Interest has not fallen nearly as much: learners still choose courses, they just do not complete the purchase.
- **Courses waiting in carts:** on 15 November, **143 learners** have courses in their cart that they never checked out, 381 courses in total. For 36 of them a course has been waiting for more than a month.

**Required training for our enterprise customers**

- **Completed on time:** three deadlines have passed this quarter. Northwind's employees completed Cloud Foundations on time in 60 of 95 cases (63%, due 10 October), Helix's completed First-Time Manager in 50 of 80 (63%, due 24 October), Corvel's completed SQL for Analysts in 39 of 60 (65%, due 3 November). In all, **149 of 235 (63%)**. It has not improved from one deadline to the next.
- **Still not completed:** on 15 November, 69 employees have not completed a required course whose deadline has passed.
- **Where they are:** most of them are in three teams: Warehouse operations at Northwind (20 of 30 employees overdue), Patient services at Helix (22 of 28) and Retail branches at Corvel (16 of 19). In the other seven teams it is 11 of 158.
- **Next deadlines:** Infrastructure as Code for Northwind on 21 November (68 of 95 employees have not completed it yet), Feedback and Difficult Conversations for Helix on 29 November (52 of 80), Data Visualization Fundamentals for Corvel on 15 December (39 of 60).
- **How we follow up today:** by hand. Before and after each deadline the team opens each employee's training record in Admin and writes to them, one at a time, with the same reminder. They sent **121 reminders** in the first period and **303** in the second, more than 80 in some weeks. Fewer than a third of the recent reminders were even opened, and the completion rate has not moved. Some employees take the same quiz several times before they pass it, or give up on it.
- **What the customers ask for:** each of the three wants to see, per team and manager, who has completed, who is at risk and who is overdue. Today we put that together by hand when they ask.

**Help and support**

- Learners asked 15 questions in the first period and 4 in the second; all but one were answered within one business day, and none is waiting today.

**Subscriptions**

- **Cancellations:** 18 in the first period and 36 in the second. Active subscriptions on 15 November: 366, about 6,950 USD in monthly recurring revenue (down 13% from September).

Price, plan, catalog, the checkout page and the Admin area stayed the same during this period. Service health was stable: we had no outages, error rates stayed very low and page speed did not change. Our regular releases went out every two weeks or so, as usual. The team is the same three people as in the summer.

## 3. What worries us

1. **Fewer learners are progressing.** About half of our paying learners now open a lesson in a given week (184 of 366). In September it was close to four in five.
2. **Learners are not completing what they start in the store.** About half of the learners who choose their next course now leave it in the cart.
3. **Our enterprise customers' required training is falling behind.** Only 63% of required courses were completed on time, and the employees who fall behind are concentrated in a few teams. Our follow-up is three people sending reminders one at a time; the number of reminders has more than doubled and the results have not changed. Two more deadlines fall in the next two weeks.
4. **Cancellations are accelerating.** Weekly cancellations doubled in the last two weeks.
5. **We do not yet understand why.** Nothing we changed on purpose explains any of this.
6. **Our capacity is limited.** Three people already handle support, operations and the training follow-up. We cannot follow up with hundreds of learners and employees one by one.

## 4. Priorities for Q4 2026

In order of importance:

1. **More learners actively progressing every week.** Get back to at least 300 learners starting or completing lessons each week, which is where we were in September (it is about 184 now).
2. **Complete the purchases learners start in the course catalog.** At least 300 learners enrolling every 30 days in a course they chose from the catalog and put in their cart (it is 158 now), and no course left waiting in a cart for more than 7 days.
3. **95% of mandatory training completed on time across our enterprise accounts.** For every deadline this quarter, at least 95% of the employees it applies to complete the required course on time (it is 63% so far), with completion visible to each customer by team and manager.

**Guardrail:** support workload must not grow. The three-person team should not handle more tickets, messages or manual tasks per week than it does today. We will judge any change against this as well as against the three goals above.

## 5. What we are asking for

We want an independent view of what is driving these numbers and which outcomes are realistic for this quarter. We are not committing to any particular approach yet. Please look at the evidence first.
