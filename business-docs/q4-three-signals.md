# LearnLoop: Q4 2026 Business Review

**Author:** Head of Product
**Date:** 15 November 2026
**Audience:** Leadership team and external advisors

## 1. Where we are

LearnLoop is an online course platform that people pay for monthly. One plan, 19 USD per month, gives access to the whole catalog: 12 self-paced courses in four areas (Data, Cloud, Leadership, Design), each made of six to ten short video lessons with quizzes.

Learners pick their next course in the store: they put it in their shopping cart and complete the checkout, which enrols them (the course is included in their plan). The cart is saved to their account, so it follows them from one device to another.

Learners who need help use the Help page: they ask the team a question there and come back to it to read the answer. We also send them a notification when the answer arrives.

We started September with about **420 paying learners**, about 7,980 USD in monthly recurring revenue. We did not run any acquisition activity this autumn, so the business today depends on the learners we already have.

The team that runs the service day to day (learner support, account questions, course operations) is **three people**. They answer every learner question themselves, from the Admin area. Product and engineering are a separate, small group.

Comparing the two most recent 30-day periods (17 Sep to 16 Oct, then 17 Oct to 15 Nov):

**Learning**

- **Learners actively taking lessons:** 340 → 195 (down about 43%). The fall happened over roughly two weeks from early October, then levelled off; it has not recovered. Most of the learners who stopped still sign in every few days and look at their dashboard and the catalog.
- **Lessons completed:** 3,402 → 1,857 (down 45%).

**The store**

- **Learners who completed a checkout:** 307 → 149 (down about 51%).
- **Learners who put a course in their cart:** 324 → 293. Interest has not fallen nearly as much: learners still choose courses, they just do not complete the purchase.
- **Courses waiting in carts:** on 15 November, **155 learners** have courses in their cart that they never checked out, 426 courses in total. For 43 of them a course has been waiting for more than a month.

**Help and support**

- **Learners who used Help:** 303 → 149 (down about 51%). Learners used to come back to Help to read their answers and to ask the next question; far fewer do now.
- **Questions asked:** 111 → 38. Almost all of them are about the same few things: certificates (37% of all questions this autumn), billing and invoices (35%) and account settings (21%). Many are close to word for word the same question.
- **Questions answered within one business day:** 78 of 111 in the first period, none of 38 in the second. Until early October a question was usually answered the same day or the next morning; in the second period the typical wait for the questions that were answered was about 12 days.
- **Questions waiting for an answer:** on 15 November, **28 questions** are open, the oldest from 14 October, and 27 of them have waited longer than one business day.

**Subscriptions**

- **Cancellations:** 18 in the first period and 36 in the second. Active subscriptions on 15 November: 366, about 6,950 USD in monthly recurring revenue (down 13% from September).

Price, plan, catalog, the checkout page and the Help page stayed the same during this period. Service health was stable: we had no outages, error rates stayed very low and page speed did not change. Our regular releases went out every two weeks or so, as usual. The team is the same three people as in the summer.

## 3. What worries us

1. **Fewer learners are progressing.** About half of our paying learners now open a lesson in a given week (188 of 366). In September it was close to four in five.
2. **Learners are not completing what they start in the store.** About half of the learners who choose their next course now leave it in the cart.
3. **Learners have stopped turning to us for help.** Half as many learners use Help as a month ago, and the questions they did ask are waiting. The questions are repetitive, but each one is still answered by hand.
4. **Cancellations are accelerating.** Weekly cancellations doubled in the last two weeks.
5. **We do not yet understand why.** Nothing we changed on purpose explains any of this.
6. **Our capacity is limited.** Three people already handle support and operations. We cannot follow up with hundreds of learners one by one.

## 4. Priorities for Q4 2026

In order of importance:

1. **More learners actively progressing every week.** Get back to at least 300 learners starting or completing lessons each week, which is where we were in September (it is about 188 now).
2. **Complete the purchases learners start.** At least 300 learners completing a checkout every 30 days by the end of the quarter (it is 149 now), and no course left waiting in a cart for more than 7 days.
3. **Answer learner questions within one business day, and keep learners using Help.** Every question answered within one business day, and at least 300 learners using Help every 30 days again (it is 149 now).

**Guardrail:** support workload must not grow. The three-person team should not handle more tickets, messages or manual tasks per week than it does today. We will judge any change against this as well as against the three goals above.

## 5. What we are asking for

We want an independent view of what is driving these numbers and which outcomes are realistic for this quarter. We are not committing to any particular approach yet. Please look at the evidence first.
