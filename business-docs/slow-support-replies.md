# LearnLoop: Q4 2026 Business Review

**Author:** Head of Product
**Date:** 15 November 2026
**Audience:** Leadership team and external advisors

## 1. Where we are

LearnLoop is an online course platform that people pay for monthly. One plan, 19 USD per month, gives access to the whole catalog: 12 self-paced courses in four areas (Data, Cloud, Leadership, Design), each made of six to ten short video lessons with quizzes.

We started September with about **420 paying learners**, about 7,980 USD in monthly recurring revenue. We did not run any acquisition activity this autumn, so the business today depends on the learners we already have.

The team that runs the service day to day (learner support, account questions, course operations) is **three people**. Product and engineering are a separate, small group.

Learners ask us questions from the Help page and read our replies there and in their notifications; the team answers from the admin console. **Our promise to learners is a reply within one business day.** A check runs every morning and flags each question that has been waiting longer than one business day; a question is flagged again every morning until someone answers it.

## 2. What happened over the last seven weeks

All figures come from our own systems. Weeks run Monday to Sunday.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| 28 Sep to 4 Oct | 6 | 6 | about 21 hours | 6 | 0 | 0 |
| 5 to 11 Oct | 6 | 3 | about 19 hours | 2 | 3 | 0 |
| 12 to 18 Oct | 18 | 4 | about 3 days | 2 | 17 | 24 |
| 19 to 25 Oct | 18 | 9 | about 6 days | 0 | 26 | 113 |
| 26 Oct to 1 Nov | 27 | 18 | about 9 days | 0 | 35 | 169 |
| 2 to 8 Nov | 23 | 26 | about 7 days | 0 | 32 | 201 |

- **Questions** went from three to six a week in September to 18 to 27 a week since mid-October. About four in five are about the same three things: **certificates** (by far the most common), **billing** and **their account**. Many of them are the same question asked by different learners.
- **Waiting time:** in September almost every question was answered the same day or the next morning. Since the second half of October no question has been answered within one business day, and the typical wait is about a week.
- **Backlog:** on 15 November, **40 questions are open**, 33 of them waiting longer than one business day; the oldest was asked 18 days ago.
- **The morning check** flagged no question at all in September (at most one a week). In the week of 2 to 8 November it flagged 201 (each question counted every morning it is still waiting).
- Everything else is stable: about 330 learners open a lesson every week, about 880 lessons are completed a week, and cancellations stay at about 4 a week. There were no outages.

## 3. What worries us

1. **We are breaking our promise to learners.** Nobody who asked a question in the last four weeks got an answer within a business day.
2. **The team cannot keep up.** Three people now spend their days on the same few questions, and the backlog keeps growing.
3. **Waiting learners are paying learners.** Several of the open questions are about billing and certificates; we do not want a slow answer to become a cancellation.

## 4. Priorities for Q4 2026

In order of importance:

1. **Answer every learner question within one business day.** We measure it with the morning check: the number of questions it flags as waiting longer than one business day, per week. In September it flagged at most 1 a week; it flagged 201 in the week of 2 to 8 November. Target: 0 within 3 months.
2. **Keep our subscribers.** Keep weekly cancellations at about 4 a week or lower.
3. **More learners actively progressing every week.** Keep at least 300 learners completing or starting lessons each week.

**Guardrail:** we will not solve this by adding people, and any change must not add manual work for the three-person team.

## 5. What we are asking for

We want an independent view of what is driving these numbers and which outcomes are realistic for this quarter. We are not committing to any particular approach yet. Please look at the evidence first.
