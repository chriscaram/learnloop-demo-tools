Mark AI agent messages unread

Opening a learner's bell during a rehearsal marks the AI agents' messages as read, so the bell loses its count and the staff screens show them as opened. This puts every message an AI agent wrote back to unread. Nothing else changes: other notifications, the AI agents' messages themselves and every other record stay as they are.
